The console's decision queue now also clears a proposal that was *dispositioned* — already
delivered, a duplicate, or absorbed into another change — rather than only one that was
approved or rejected. Those need no review either, and they were sitting on the mandatory
list indefinitely.

A disposition is honoured only from the agent that owns the disposition record. Any other
sender's message is ignored, deliberately: a review that silently vanishes from your queue
is a worse failure than one that lingers, because nothing shows it happened.
