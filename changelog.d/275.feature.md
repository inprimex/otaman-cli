`otaman doctor` and `otaman policy critics` now warn when your verification-gate
configuration leaves an agent's own proposals with no reviewer at all. That happens
with a natural-looking pairing — reviewers chosen by affected repo, falling back to
sensitivity — because an agent is never its own critic and the fallback then selects
nobody; previously you only found out as `no-eligible-critic` at review time, with no
hint that the cause was the configuration. The warning names the agents affected and
how to fix it. Relatedly, a hook whose policy this command cannot evaluate from a
checkout now says so instead of reporting that it selected nobody.
