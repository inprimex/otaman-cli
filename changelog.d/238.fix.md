A `spec-change` notification now says why it chose its recipients. When no repo
owner could be resolved it fell back to notifying spec-agent and the human — which
is the right routing — but the message then told the reader the change carried no
repo annotations. That was false in every case except one, and false in the case
that matters most: a mistyped `@otaman-<repo>` in `tasks.md` routes the whole
dispatch away from the agent who owns the work, and the human was told the change
assigns nobody.

Four different problems produced the same notification and the same wrong
explanation: a mistyped annotation, a `repos:` key of the wrong shape, `repos:`
entries that name no owners, and no `repos:` at all. Each now names itself, in the
message and in what `otaman notify-change` prints, including the annotation text
that did not match. The recipients themselves are unchanged.
