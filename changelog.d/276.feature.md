`otaman policy critics` and `otaman doctor` now distinguish a critic selection they
**could not evaluate** from one that **selected nobody**. A hook whose policy needs an
input only a live gate supplies (such as `target_role`) is reported as
`could not know who this selects — target_role is not available from a checkout`,
naming the missing input, instead of showing an empty critic list as though the policy
had decided. The two states read differently because they mean opposite things: one is
a limit of what a checkout can see, the other is a real gap in your configuration.

Both surfaces also show the new top-level `roles:` table from `verification-gates.yaml`,
so when a `role-based` selection does come up empty you can see the table it chose from.
A `verification-gates.yaml` your version of otaman refuses to parse is still reported as
`NOT CHECKED` with the reason, never as an absent policy.
