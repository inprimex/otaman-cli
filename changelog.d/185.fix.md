`otaman doctor` now fails a registered repo that is not under version control.
Previously a plain directory carrying otaman's two marker files was
indistinguishable from a real checkout, so repos that had never been
initialised reported `[OK]` — work could be dispatched into them for weeks with
no history and no way to recover it. Worktree and submodule checkouts, and
repos nested inside a parent checkout, are correctly recognised as versioned.
Doctor also notes when a repo declares no remote, since without one there is no
push and no recovery on another machine.
