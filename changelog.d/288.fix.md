Approved and rejected proposals no longer linger as if they were still waiting for you.

`otaman check` looked for the proposal's identifier in the subject line of the approval
announcement, while the announcement writes it in the body — so an approval was never
found and every decided proposal kept reporting "waiting for human approval", in one case
for two days after it was approved.

The console's decision queue had the matching half: it treated a proposal as outstanding
unless it carried an acknowledgement file, and a decision recorded as an announcement
leaves no such file. On this program that queue showed 19 items of which 14 had already
been decided — a mandatory-review list that is mostly resolved items teaches you to stop
reading it.

Both surfaces now read the same back-link, written and parsed in one place so they cannot
disagree again about what "decided" means.
