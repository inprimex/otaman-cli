Knowledge entries can now be corrected. `otaman knowledge amend <entry>` records
a correction that supersedes an earlier entry without rewriting or deleting it,
so the original stays readable as the record of what was believed at the time.
Anyone who opens the superseded entry is told a correction exists and which one
to read; the correction names what it corrects.

`list` gains the same lifecycle the entries now carry: it shows active entries
by default, flags ones that have been amended, and can be narrowed to a
partition, a state, or a row budget — and says so whenever a filter or budget
hides something rather than leaving you to wonder.
