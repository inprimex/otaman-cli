Console decisions are now recoverable. Every confirmation names what it is about
to act on — the artifact type, its title and who sent it — instead of just the
verb, so you can see which item you are deferring before you defer it. Press `u`
to undo the last decision when it has no consequence outside the console; when
it does (an approval that has already broadcast, a ratify agents are acting on),
undo refuses and says which consequence already left. Ctrl+A shows what you have
done this session, with the target and outcome of each action.
