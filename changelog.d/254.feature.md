`otaman policy routes` now sees the agents you actually declared. It read
`platform.yaml` alone, so a program whose agents live in `.agents/agents.yaml` — the
normal case — saw "no agents declared" and the doctor's effective-routing check had
nothing to show. Declarations are now read from either place, and the listing says
which. New: `otaman policy routes --declare <agent> --family F [--model M] [--local]`
writes an agent's route for you, validated by the same resolver the dispatch uses, in
place and with your comments intact, and says so plainly when the route is already
what you asked for.
