Outcome and solution history now records every field a step changed, not just one of
them: accepting a cost shows the solution, the acceptance and the status move in a
single entry, and a first-time choice no longer reads as though it replaced a null
one. Decisions made under a role also record which role authorised them. Console
panels read registries through the same shared path as the write verbs, and a
display read can no longer be written back over your comments.
