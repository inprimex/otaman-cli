The spec-proposal gate's verdict now shows where reviewers look. A proposal whose
critique has run carries it on the console queue row, and `otaman spec status` names
the verdict, the critic, and which pass produced it.

The gate comments; it does not block. A failing verdict renders as information, with
no refusal and no change to any exit code, and the rendering says so — a reviewer who
sees "fail" needs to know nothing is holding the proposal, or they wait for an
approval that was never blocked.

Where no critique has run, the surface says "not critiqued" rather than leaving a
blank, since a blank where a verdict goes is indistinguishable from a pass.

Stage 1's 0-100 score renders the same way once something produces it; today nothing
does, and the surface says "not scored" rather than showing a zero.
