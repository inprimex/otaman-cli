`otaman cleanup --purge` now tells you how much of what it is about to delete exists
nowhere else. Instead of "unrecoverable unless this directory is version-controlled"
— a condition you had to check yourself — it counts, per archive month, how many
messages are missing from git HEAD and says plainly that deleting those is permanent.
A month it cannot check (no repo, no commits yet) is reported as unrecoverable rather
than assumed safe, and the dry run states the same figure as the real run.
