The artifact tree gains a `:` command line for filtering. Type `:p1` for
priority, `:sappr` for a status (prefixes resolve, and an ambiguous one lists
the candidates rather than guessing), `:t login` to search, and `:a` for
everything waiting on you — combine them by typing them together. `:e+` and
`:e-` expand or collapse everything within whatever filter is active. The
active filter is shown, and Esc clears it before leaving the screen.

Rows waiting on you are now marked in the tree, and the header says how many
there are.
