`otaman doctor` gained a knowledge-vault section, and `otaman knowledge sweep`
retires entries that have gone stale. The sweep reports by default and moves
nothing unless you pass `--apply`; entries go *dormant*, never deleted, and
`--restore` brings one back. Doctor still never modifies files — it points at
the sweep.

`otaman knowledge add --domain <term>` now validates the term against the
program's vocabulary registry. An undeclared term is refused and the refusal
names the registry path; when no registry exists the term is accepted and the
output says plainly that it went unvalidated.
