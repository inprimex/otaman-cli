New: `otaman spec sweep` applies filed `task-complete` ticks to `tasks.md`. It
reports by default and writes only with `--apply`, counts what it applied and
what was already done, and says so out loud when it found nothing — a silent
sweep cannot be told from one that never ran. Filings that were withdrawn (the
task was un-ticked after they were filed) are not re-applied, filings naming
nothing readable are listed rather than dropped, and when the reader is
unavailable it reports NOT CHECKED instead of zero.
