Choosing a solution and funding it are now separate decisions. Where the hats are
held by different people, `otaman outcome accept-cost <id> --solution <SOL>` refuses
and names the two verbs to run instead — `otaman outcome choose` (the CTO's call),
then `otaman outcome accept-cost` (the CEO's). Where one person holds both hats, the
one-step form still works, and the audit log records both decisions with the hat
behind each, so the record shows what was decided even when the keystroke did not.
