Acking a task-assignment no longer reports you as working on someone else's
task. Being CC'd on another agent's assignment left your status claiming their
work; and because the parser did not recognise the format the dispatcher
actually emits, acking your own assignment recorded `working` with no task at
all — the state that renders as `working (—)` and cannot be told apart from a
dead session. Your status now names the task you were actually given.
