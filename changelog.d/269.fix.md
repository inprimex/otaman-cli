`otaman blocked clear` can now clear a task that was waiting on another agent, not
just one waiting on a proposal. Those entries are identified by their change name,
and the command only ever looked at the proposal field — so the entries `otaman
blocked` writes for a dependency wait could not be cleared by it at all, leaving
hand-editing the register as the only way out. Pass either the proposal stem or the
change name. An unmatched clear now leaves the file completely untouched.
