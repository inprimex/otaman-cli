New `otaman knowledge add|list|show` records the durable operational facts a
team accumulates — a lesson, a decision, a measured number — so they outlive the
session that learned them. Every entry must carry evidence someone can check
later (a file and line, a message, a number); an entry without one is refused,
and the refusal says why. Entries carry a review date, and `list` flags the ones
that are overdue rather than letting them be trusted forever.
