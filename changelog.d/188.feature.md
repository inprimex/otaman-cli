The artifact tree now says what you are looking at and answers keys
consistently. Each lens carries a one-line header naming the question it
answers, arrow keys expand and collapse while Enter only opens detail, and
Enter now works in the lifecycle view where it previously did nothing. Rows
that had been decided out are no longer rendered so faintly that they read as
blank lines, and the read panel shows a row's description and the outcomes or
solutions it points at.
